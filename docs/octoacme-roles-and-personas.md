# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interaction with other roles
- Work closely with Product Managers to align roadmap priorities with execution timelines
- Coordinate with Developers to confirm delivery sequencing, dependencies, and blockers
- Partner with stakeholders to communicate scope, risks, and milestone progress

---

## Project Sponsors

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors provide strategic direction, funding, and executive sponsorship for the initiative. They ensure the project aligns with business priorities and that key decisions are made with the right level of authority.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm project goals, scope, and expected business value
- Approve funding, staffing, and major milestones
- Support prioritization when trade-offs are required
- Remove organizational obstacles that block progress
- Review executive-level status and key risks

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers measurable business value
- Maintain alignment between delivery teams and organizational strategy
- Provide clear sponsorship and decision support for critical issues

### Typical Communication
- Steering committee updates and leadership briefings
- Decision points for scope, funding, and escalation
- High-level reviews of progress, risks, and strategic outcomes

### Interaction with existing roles
- Partners with Project Managers to define success criteria, major milestones, and escalation paths
- Works with Product Managers to confirm that the delivered outcome supports business goals
- Relies on Developers and Delivery Leads to provide objective progress and risk updates before decision-making

---

## Delivery Leads

### Role Summary
Delivery Leads oversee execution across teams and ensure work is sequenced, resourced, and ready for delivery. They translate priorities into practical plans and coordinate cross-functional dependencies.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate daily execution across product, engineering, and operations teams
- Sequence work and manage dependencies across delivery streams
- Monitor progress against plan and highlight delivery risks early
- Support capacity planning and work balancing across contributors
- Ensure handoffs and delivery checkpoints are completed as expected

### Goals
- Maintain predictable execution and delivery flow
- Reduce bottlenecks and cross-team confusion
- Improve the quality and consistency of project delivery

### Typical Communication
- Delivery reviews and dependency planning sessions
- Status updates with Project Managers and team leads
- Escalation conversations for risks, blockers, and resourcing gaps

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works alongside Project Managers to monitor timelines and execution risks
- Coordinates with Developers to translate priorities into actionable sprint and milestone plans
- Provides Product Managers with practical execution trade-offs and dependency impacts

---

## Stakeholder Managers / Communicators

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Managers maintain alignment with the people and groups impacted by the project. They help ensure that the right messages, approvals, and feedback loops are in place across the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and map stakeholder interests, influence, and communication needs
- Manage stakeholder updates, approvals, and escalation paths
- Facilitate communications across leadership, partners, and delivery teams
- Help resolve concerns that may affect adoption, expectations, or delivery confidence
- Keep key stakeholders informed of changes in scope, schedule, or risk

### Goals
- Maintain trust and transparency across stakeholders
- Ensure decision-makers have the information they need at the right time
- Reduce misalignment and avoid preventable escalations

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings and governance updates
- Change communications and milestone announcements
- Feedback capture and issue triage with leads and managers

### Interaction with existing roles
- Supports Project Managers by tailoring updates for the right audiences and timelines
- Works with Product Managers to ensure stakeholder feedback informs roadmap decisions
- Coordinates with Delivery Leads to communicate changes that affect timelines, dependencies, or scope

---

## Project Operations / Admin Support

### Role Summary
Project Operations / Admin Support ensures the project has the documentation, structure, and coordination needed to run smoothly. They help keep governance, meeting rhythms, and follow-up actions organized.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain project documentation, artifact libraries, and meeting records
- Track action items, decisions, and follow-up owners
- Support scheduling, logistics, and recurring meeting cadence
- Keep risk logs, issue trackers, and status materials current
- Help teams stay aligned on standard operating practices and templates

### Goals
- Reduce administrative friction and reporting gaps
- Support project consistency and traceability
- Improve team readiness for decisions, reviews, and handoffs

### Typical Communication
- Meeting notes, action trackers, and status summaries
- Document updates and operational coordination requests
- Support communications with project leads and stakeholder groups

### Interaction with existing roles
- Enables Project Managers to keep stakeholder updates and project artifacts consistent
- Supports Delivery Leads by tracking actions, decisions, and blockers across the team
- Assists Product Managers and Developers by maintaining shared documentation and planning artifacts

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These additional roles help clarify accountability, communication paths, and decision ownership across project delivery.

---

## Summary
The OctoAcme roles model is stronger when it includes not only delivery and product responsibilities, but also strategic sponsorship, execution leadership, stakeholder communication, and operational support. This provides teams with clearer accountability, more predictable collaboration, and stronger project outcomes.
